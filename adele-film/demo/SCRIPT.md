# Adele Care demo film: "One week at Riverside Dental" (script v3, about 4:15)

For dentists and practice owners, to share instead of explaining Adele every time. Narrated by Adele in the
first person (the voice of the 90-second film). No captions.

**The story.** We follow one practice through one week. Act 1: the phone rings at 8am with nobody there to
answer, and every gap in the week costs a chair. Act 2: Monday to Friday, each day brings a real problem and
Adele solves it in front of us, while a "Chairs filled this week" counter in the corner keeps climbing.
Act 3: Friday at five, the owner opens the weekly report, sees what the week was worth, and stays in control.

**Three messages.** More revenue (recall, cancellations filled, confirmed appointments). You stay in control.
Full NHS compliance and cybersecurity protocols are planned.

**Sources.** Every capability comes from the Adele Care brochure and the "Adele Care Phase 1 prototype"
specification. Names, times and counts are illustrative (the counter is labelled "Example week"). No £ figures.

---

## Act 1: the empty reception (0:00-0:36)

**1. Cold open (0:00-0:14).** Dim reception, chairs empty, a desk phone ringing. On screen: "Monday 8:00 ·
Reception opens 8:30". The ring keeps going.

> It's eight o'clock on Monday morning. Reception doesn't open for half an hour, and the phone is already ringing.

**2. What it costs (0:14-0:36).** The ring cuts to silence. Three things appear one by one and drift into an
empty chair: a missed call, a cancelled 2pm, an overdue recall list. Then the Adele symbol lights the screen.

> Every unanswered call, every cancellation left empty, every recall nobody had time to send, is a chair that
> sits empty. Your team is with patients. They can't be everywhere. I can. I'm Adele, the AI receptionist for
> UK dental practices. This is one week at Riverside Dental.

## Act 2: the week (0:36-3:30)

**3. How I work (0:36-0:56).** Phone, WhatsApp, SMS, email and web chat flow into Adele ("Practice knowledge
base · your booking rules"), then out to your PMS diary, notes, Stripe payments, your team and a live
dashboard. A patient card shows one history across every channel.

> Patients reach me by phone, WhatsApp, SMS, email or web chat, and I keep one history for every patient. I
> follow your booking rules, update your PMS, take payments through Stripe, and keep your team in the loop.

**4. MONDAY 8:00: the call nobody was there for (0:56-1:38).** Back to that ringing phone. This time it's
answered. Live call with transcript; the diary fills; cards: Registered · Added to your diary · Deposit
taken (Stripe) · Summary and next action in notes. Counter 1.

> Adele (call): Good morning, Riverside Dental, this is Adele. How can I help?
> Patient (call): Hi, I've just moved to the area. Can I register and book a check-up?
> Adele (call): Of course. I have Wednesday at nine thirty. Shall I book that?
> Patient (call): Yes please.
> Adele (call): Done. I'll send you a secure link for the deposit, with directions and parking.
>
> Narration: Registered, booked, and the deposit taken, before reception opens. Your team arrives to a call
> summary and next step in the patient's notes. And anything urgent, I pass straight to them.

**5. MONDAY 9:12: the enquiry that would have gone cold (1:38-2:00).** A website enquiry lands while the team
is busy. Within minutes: Adele calls back, then the brochure's WhatsApp exchange books Thursday 4:15 with a
deposit. Counter 2, 3.

> At twelve minutes past nine, a website enquiry comes in while everyone's busy. I call back within minutes.
> On WhatsApp it's the same: I offer times from your diary, book the one they choose, and take the deposit.

**6. TUESDAY 13:05: a 2pm cancellation (2:00-2:22).** The 2pm slot turns red. Adele offers it by WhatsApp to
the waitlist you choose (tabs: Private · Kids); Sarah replies "Yes please"; the slot turns blue. Counter 4.

> Tuesday. A two o'clock cancels an hour before. I offer the slot to the waitlist you've chosen, by WhatsApp
> or call, and the first patient to say yes is moved in. The chair stays full.

**7. WEDNESDAY 7:40: the hygienist is off sick (2:22-2:40).** Eight appointments in the day's list; one by one
each is offered a new time and moves. Counter: "8 appointments kept".

> Wednesday. Your hygienist is off sick. Before the first patient leaves home, I've contacted every one of them
> and moved them to a new time.

**8. THURSDAY: the patient who hasn't confirmed (2:40-3:08).** The four steps from the brochure light up in
turn: When booked (reminder, directions, parking) · 2 days before (WhatsApp Yes/No, SMS optional) · 1 day
before (automated call) · Still no reply (flagged to your team). Friday's 9:30 patient walks the path and is
flagged to reception. A missed visit is followed up and rebooked.

> Every appointment is confirmed. When it's booked, I send a reminder with directions and parking. Two days
> before, I ask on WhatsApp: yes or no? No reply, and I call the day before. Still nothing, and I flag it to
> your team. If someone does miss their visit, I follow up and rebook them, under your own DNA policy.

**9. FRIDAY: recall (3:08-3:30).** "64 patients overdue for hygiene." Messages go out by call, WhatsApp and
email; bookings tick in; the counter climbs. A completed hygiene visit triggers a whitening suggestion email.
Lapsed patients get the offer you set. A welfare check and Google review prompt follow each visit.

> Friday is recall day, and recall is where most of your revenue is waiting. Patients overdue for check-ups
> and hygiene hear from me by call, WhatsApp and email. After a hygiene visit, I can suggest treatments like
> whitening. Lapsed patients are invited back with the offers you set.

## Act 3: what the week was worth (3:30-4:15)

**10. FRIDAY 17:00: the weekly report (3:30-3:50).** The owner's dashboard opens: New patient enquiries by
channel · Bookings converted · Chase success rate · Revenue generated. The counter settles on the week's total.

> Five o'clock on Friday. Every enquiry by channel, every booking converted, and the revenue I generated, in
> one report. One week. Every call answered. More chairs filled.

**11. You stay in control (3:50-4:04).** Toggles flip: Missed enquiry follow-up (on) · Recall campaigns (on) ·
Lead chasing (manual) · Lab work tracking (on). A staff notification: "Please chase: Mr Patel".

> And you're always in control. Every task runs automatically or manually, your choice. I follow your rules,
> I tell your team when something needs a person, and I track your lab work too.

**12. Built to be trusted (4:04-4:12).** Checklist: Call recording you control · No clinical outcome data
collected · Follows your rules and policies. Then a card marked "Planned": Full NHS compliance ·
Cybersecurity protocols.

> Call recording is yours to control, and I never collect clinical outcome data. We're working towards full
> NHS compliance, with cybersecurity protocols to match.

**13. Invitation (4:12-4:24).** The empty reception from the opening, now calm; the logo builds;
"Pilot places from December 2026 are limited"; adelecare.co.uk.

> See me on a real practice day. Pilot places from December 2026 are limited. Apply at adelecare dot co dot uk.

## Accuracy notes

- Capabilities shown, all from the brochure or prototype spec: inbound calls with urgent and overflow calls
  passed to staff; call summary and next action written to notes; booking, cancelling and rescheduling in
  the PMS; payments through Stripe; one history across channels; new patient registration; reminders with
  directions and parking; two-step confirmation (WhatsApp 2 days before, automated call 1 day before,
  flagged if no reply); waitlists chosen by the practice, with groups such as kids and private; rescheduling
  when a clinician is off; DNA follow-up under practice rules; missed enquiries called or texted back within
  minutes; recall by call, WhatsApp and email with a detailed report; treatment suggestions such as
  whitening after hygiene; offers for lapsed patients; welfare check, survey and Google review prompt;
  automatic or manual per task; staff notified to chase; dashboard of enquiries, chase success, conversions
  and revenue; lab work tracking.
- "Your PMS" throughout; Dentally is not named. Stripe is named.
- Compliance is a plan ("working towards", "Planned"), never presented as certification.
- Riverside Dental, Sarah, Mr Patel, times and counts are illustrative. Phone numbers use Ofcom's 07700 900xxx
  drama range.
